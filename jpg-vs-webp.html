JPG vs WebP: Key Differences

JPG and WebP are common image formats. The best choice depends on the image, the software that must open it, and whether transparency is needed.

JPG / JPEG

JPG is widely supported and commonly used for photographs. It uses lossy compression and does not support transparent pixels.

WebP

WebP supports lossy and lossless compression and can preserve transparency. It often produces smaller files at similar visual quality, but confirm the receiving software supports it.

Which should you choose?

  • Choose JPG when broad compatibility is the priority.
  • Try WebP for web use when smaller file sizes are helpful.
  • Keep transparency when your image needs a transparent background.
