Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Image processing

The image compression feature processes selected files locally in your browser. This website does not upload image files to an image-processing server.

The hosting provider may process standard technical information, such as request logs, IP addresses, browser details, and timestamps, to deliver and protect the website. The host's current policy governs this processing.

Cookies and third-party services

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to measure page views, interactions, traffic sources, and general audience usage. Google Analytics may collect technical information such as pages visited, browser and device information, approximate location, and interaction data, and may use cookies or similar technologies. Google processes this information according to its privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy. Images selected in the compressor are processed locally in your browser and are not sent to Google Analytics by this website.

External links

This website may link to external sites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue through the project support page. Do not include private images or sensitive information in a public report.
